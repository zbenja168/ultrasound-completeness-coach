# Bench session checklist (one page)

Written 2026-10-30 for the second session. Everything here follows from what the 2026-10-22 recordings showed
(`docs/BENCH_2026-10-22_RESULTS.md`). The full runbook is `docs/BENCH_DAY.md`.

## Bring

- [ ] Laptop with the repo pulled today and the `.venv` working (`python scripts/record_us_only.py --help` runs).
- [ ] Cam Link 4K + HDMI cable (worked last time; device name "Cam Link 4k" via DirectShow).
- [ ] Marker cube: WHITE body, markers from `docs/print/markers_35mm.pdf` (or 30 mm) cut on the grey line with the
      white margin kept, ids on the faces as labelled. Measure one black square and write the size down.
- [ ] Stalk and clip to hold the cube above the probe body, clear of the fingers.
- [ ] Phone (charged, charger), tripod. Lock focus and exposure if the camera app allows, 1080p, highest frame rate.
- [ ] Chessboard page (page 2 of the same PDF) taped flat to something rigid (clipboard, book).
- [ ] Ruler or tape measure, and a straight edge or rail to slide along (a book spine or a ruler taped to the
      phantom surface is enough). Pen and paper for notes.
- [ ] Pin or bead on a thread, a small container of water (pinhead calibration). Skip only if the lab cannot do it.
- [ ] Gel, tape, a spare USB cable.

## Set up (10 min)

1. Cart: same preset as last time if possible (C1-5, depth 16 cm) so `screen.json` carries over. Any change to
   depth or zoom: note it and freeze a frame with the depth ruler visible for a new crop.
2. Phone on the tripod, lens 25-30 cm from where the probe will be, looking down at about 45 degrees. Check on
   the phone screen that TWO cube faces are visible in the normal scanning grip. Nobody touches the tripod after.
3. Film the chessboard for 30 s: tilt it left, right, towards and away from the phone, near and far, fill the
   frame. Not flat on the table.
4. Photograph the cube on the probe next to a ruler, from two sides. Measure cube centre to probe face (mm) and
   write it down.

## Takes (same names as last time, three sharp taps at the start of every take)

| Take | What | Pass sign |
| --- | --- | --- |
| practice | 20 s sweep, look at the phone preview | cube faces not covered by fingers |
| jiggle | probe on phantom, 5-6 sharp taps over 20 s | used for sync, worked last time |
| pinhead | rock and tilt about the pin in water, 30 degrees each way, 20-30 s, pin bright | pin stays in the image |
| slide | slide along the rail, HOLD 1 s at each mark: 0, 50, 0, 100, 0, 50, 0, 100 mm | marks written in notes |
| full | complete sweep of the phantom region | 30-60 s, steady |
| partial | same sweep, stop at about 60 % | |
| blocked | full sweep, hand over the phone lens for 3 s in the middle | |

Keep the cube in the phone's view during the slide holds. If a take goes wrong, record it again with the same name;
the recorder numbers them.

## Before leaving (5 min)

- [ ] `notes.txt` in the session folder: preset and depth, marker size measured, cube-to-probe-face distance,
      slide distances, which take of each name is the keeper, anything odd.
- [ ] Photos of the rig (cube on probe, phone position) into the folder.
- [ ] Phone clips copied, or uploaded to Drive the same day, with the laptop folder.

## What is different from 2026-10-22, in one line each

White-margined markers on a white cube on a stalk; phone 3x closer and at 45 degrees seeing two faces; chessboard
tilted, not flat; pinhead take recorded; slide distances and cube distance written down; photos taken.
